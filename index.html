Methodology

What this simulator measures

This is a rule-based educational simulation, not real biometric eye tracking.

Task-based scan flow

Checks whether the page supports product identification, offer evaluation, option selection, reassurance, and a reachable purchase action. Missing or deeply buried critical stages cap the score.

Gestalt grouping

Related information is easier to understand when it is spatially close and grouped.

Visual crowding and occlusion

Overlaps reduce recognition. Hidden critical content cannot earn a strong score.

Spatial expectations

Price, variants, sizing help, reassurance, and actions are evaluated against learned product-page expectations.

Initial viewport and action availability

Discoverability decays with depth. A primary CTA placed far below the decision zone receives a strong task penalty; a sticky CTA can only partially compensate.

Simulated heatmap

The heatmap combines task importance, visibility, viewport depth, semantic relationships, crowding, and action readiness. It is illustrative, not biometric.

Evidence boundaries

The model uses continuous heuristic indices, not conversion probabilities. Exact pixel weights are calibration choices for comparison and are not claimed as universal biological constants.

Reference foundation

Core concepts are informed by research on visual crowding and critical spacing, perceptual grouping in HCI, task-dependent visual search, and eye-tracking findings on scrolling and attention.
