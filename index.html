Methodology

What the simulation uses

Spatial expectations

Common interface conventions inform where users are likely to search for familiar controls. The simulation rewards conventional relationships such as price near title and CTA after selection controls.

Gestalt proximity & grouping

Objects placed near one another are more likely to be perceived as related. The tool checks distances among product information, variants and the action.

Visual crowding

Recognition becomes harder when multiple objects compete within a small region. The tool penalizes overlapping and tightly packed elements.

Gaze cueing

Observed eye or head direction can orient attention toward the gazed-at location. The model-image element can point toward another element and modestly boost it.

Task-based sequence

The path is modeled around a shopping task: identify the product, evaluate key information, select an option, then act. This is a design hypothesis, not a universal biological reading pattern.
