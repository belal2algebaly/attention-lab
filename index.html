METHODOLOGY

What this tool actually measures

This is a rule-based educational simulation, not real eye tracking.

Product Flow

Checks whether the page supports a practical shopping sequence: identify the product, understand it, evaluate price and options, then reach the primary action.

Grouping & Visibility

Checks whether related information is close enough to read as one unit and whether any element is partially or fully covered.

Gutenberg

An optional historical layout heuristic. It is included for comparison only and is not treated as a universal biological law.

Hard rule

Critical content cannot receive a good score when it is missing or obscured. Price and Add to Cart are treated as task-critical.
